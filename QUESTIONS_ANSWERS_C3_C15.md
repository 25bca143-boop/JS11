# Questions and Answers

C3. Count titles and copies by category.
Answer: GROUP BY, COUNT and SUM.

C4. Top 5 borrowed books.
Answer: JOIN, GROUP BY, ORDER BY and LIMIT.

C5. Members who never borrowed.
Answer: LEFT JOIN and IS NULL.

C6. Currently issued books.
Answer: JOIN and return_date IS NULL.

C7. Late returns and fines.
Answer: DATEDIFF and Rs. 2 per late day.

C8. Total fine per member.
Answer: SUM, GROUP BY and HAVING.

C9. Student vs Faculty usage.
Answer: JOIN and GROUP BY.

C10. Rank members.
Answer: RANK() OVER.

C11. Popular category.
Answer: JOIN and GROUP BY.

C12. Zero copies and pending loans.
Answer: Subquery and IN.

C13. Member history.
Answer: Stored procedure.

C14. Reduce available copies.
Answer: AFTER INSERT trigger.

C15. Recommendations.
Answer:
Buy more popular books.
Remind members about overdue books.
Send reminders before due dates.
Encourage timely returns.
